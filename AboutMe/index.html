A little about me

I make games
from the inside out.

I'm Alvaro, a game programmer from Valencia, Spain. I love figuring out how things work, then building them myself.

My work spans gameplay, graphics, and game AI. I studied Computer Science for Games at Sheffield Hallam University after developing Sun Warriors with the team at ESAT in Valencia.

From rendering technology to player-facing effects and intelligent agents, I enjoy turning technical ideas into things people can see, play, and explore.
