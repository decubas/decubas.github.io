Team Squad AI

An AI project exploring behavior and decision-making in capture the flag.

Demo in Engine

Download Demo

This final-year project at Sheffield Hallam University explored an AI agent for Capture the Flag. Built in Unity3D, the agent used behaviour trees and a team-squad structure for movement and commands.

The original goal was to create two AI teams that played Capture the Flag on the same layout, with enough variation to make each match different. Because of the project's complexity and limited time, I narrowed the scope to focus on the core AI and dropped squad tactics.

Initial project goals included:

  • Investigate modern AI algorithms for games (Decision tree, finite-state machine)
  • Develop a base system for the AI
  • Investigate pathfinding and squad movement
  • Develop a base movement system
  • Divide the AI into two branches: Attack & Defense
  • Develop base finite-state machine AI
  • Develop base Defense AI
  • Create a base Demo with two teams and capture the flag
  • Research Teamwork AI
  • Develop and implement Teamwork AI
